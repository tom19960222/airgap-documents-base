A remote cluster is a [cluster](index.md#cluster) that
connects to a [control plane](index.md#control-plane)
residing outside of the cluster. A remote cluster can connect to a control plane
running in a [primary cluster](index.md#primary-cluster)
or to an [external control plane](index.md#external-control-plane).
