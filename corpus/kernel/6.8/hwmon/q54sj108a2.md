## Description

This driver implements support for DELTA Q54SJ108A2NCAH, Q54SJ108A2NCDH,
Q54SJ108A2NCPG, and Q54SJ108A2NCPH 1/4 Brick DC/DC Regulated Power Module
with PMBus support.

The driver is a client driver to the core PMBus driver.
Please see [Kernel driver pmbus](pmbus.md) for details on PMBus client drivers.

## Usage Notes

This driver does not auto-detect devices. You will have to instantiate the
devices explicitly. Please see [How to instantiate I2C devices](../i2c/instantiating-devices.md) for
details.
