## [Plugin Index](index.md#id2)

These are the plugins in the cisco.asa collection:

### Modules

- [asa_acls module](asa_acls_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-asa-asa-acls-module) – Access-Lists resource module
- [asa_command module](asa_command_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-asa-asa-command-module) – Run arbitrary commands on Cisco ASA devices
- [asa_config module](asa_config_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-asa-asa-config-module) – Manage configuration sections on Cisco ASA devices
- [asa_facts module](asa_facts_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-asa-asa-facts-module) – Collect facts from remote devices running Cisco ASA
