## [Plugin Index](index.md#id2)

These are the plugins in the community.grafana collection:

### Modules

- [grafana_dashboard module](grafana_dashboard_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-dashboard-module) – Manage Grafana dashboards
- [grafana_datasource module](grafana_datasource_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-datasource-module) – Manage Grafana datasources
- [grafana_folder module](grafana_folder_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-folder-module) – Manage Grafana Folders
- [grafana_notification_channel module](grafana_notification_channel_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-notification-channel-module) – Manage Grafana Notification Channels
- [grafana_organization module](grafana_organization_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-organization-module) – Manage Grafana Organization
- [grafana_organization_user module](grafana_organization_user_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-organization-user-module) – Manage Grafana Organization Users.
- [grafana_plugin module](grafana_plugin_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-plugin-module) – Manage Grafana plugins via grafana-cli
- [grafana_team module](grafana_team_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-team-module) – Manage Grafana Teams
- [grafana_user module](grafana_user_module.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-user-module) – Manage Grafana User

### Callback Plugins

- [grafana_annotations callback](grafana_annotations_callback.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-annotations-callback) – send ansible events as annotations on charts to grafana over http api.

### Lookup Plugins

- [grafana_dashboard lookup](grafana_dashboard_lookup.md#ansible-collections-community-grafana-grafana-dashboard-lookup) – list or search grafana dashboards
