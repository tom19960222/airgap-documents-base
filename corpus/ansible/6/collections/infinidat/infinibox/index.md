# Infinidat.Infinibox

Collection version 1.3.12

- [Description](index.md#description)
- [Plugin Index](index.md#plugin-index)

## [Description](index.md#id1)

A set of Ansible modules that allow simple, but powerful, idempotent interactions with Infinidat InfiniBoxes.

**Authors:**

- Infinidat <[partners.infi@infinidat.com](mailto:partners.infi%40infinidat.com)>
- David Ohlemacher
- Gregory Shulov

**Supported ansible-core versions:**

- 2.9.10 or newer

[Repository (Sources)](https://www.github.com/infinidat/ansible-infinidat-collection)

## [Plugin Index](index.md#id2)

These are the plugins in the infinidat.infinibox collection:

### Modules

- [infini_cluster module](infini_cluster_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-cluster-module) – Create, Delete and Modify Host Cluster on Infinibox
- [infini_export module](infini_export_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-export-module) – Create, Delete or Modify NFS Exports on Infinibox
- [infini_export_client module](infini_export_client_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-export-client-module) – Create, Delete or Modify NFS Client(s) for existing exports on Infinibox
- [infini_fs module](infini_fs_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-fs-module) – Create, Delete or Modify filesystems on Infinibox
- [infini_host module](infini_host_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-host-module) – Create, Delete or Modify Hosts on Infinibox
- [infini_map module](infini_map_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-map-module) – Create and Delete mapping of a volume to a host or cluster on Infinibox
- [infini_network_space module](infini_network_space_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-network-space-module) – Create, Delete and Modify network spaces on Infinibox
- [infini_pool module](infini_pool_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-pool-module) – Create, Delete and Modify Pools on Infinibox
- [infini_port module](infini_port_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-port-module) – Add and Delete fiber channel and iSCSI ports to a host on Infinibox
- [infini_user module](infini_user_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-user-module) – Create, Delete and Modify a User on Infinibox
- [infini_vol module](infini_vol_module.md#ansible-collections-infinidat-infinibox-infini-vol-module) – Create, Delete or Modify volumes on Infinibox
