## [Plugin Index](index.md#id2)

These are the plugins in the cisco.nso collection:

### Modules

- [nso_action module](nso_action_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-nso-nso-action-module) – Executes Cisco NSO actions and verifies output.
- [nso_config module](nso_config_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-nso-nso-config-module) – Manage Cisco NSO configuration and service synchronization.
- [nso_query module](nso_query_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-nso-nso-query-module) – Query data from Cisco NSO.
- [nso_show module](nso_show_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-nso-nso-show-module) – Displays data from Cisco NSO.
- [nso_verify module](nso_verify_module.md#ansible-collections-cisco-nso-nso-verify-module) – Verifies Cisco NSO configuration.
